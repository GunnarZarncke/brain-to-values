Battery E pre-registration (frozen 2026-10-22, before any rating, vignette text or interview)

PURPOSE
Test whether the carrier of value change depends on the kind of practice:
elite example for moral practices, peer conformity for practical ones.
This pattern was noticed after the fact in batteries A-D; battery E tests it on new domains.

CELLS (10 domains each)
moral_real, moral_fictional, practical_real, practical_fictional.
Candidates and their priority order are in candidates.csv (13 per cell).
In each cell the first 10 candidates, in priority order, that pass both the source check
and the classification rule are used. If fewer than 10 pass, the cell runs short and this is reported.

SOURCE CHECK
Real domains:
  (1) Dated US evidence shows B was a minority, illegal or stigmatized practice around 1930
      (1925-1945) and a majority or broadly accepted practice now (2010 or later).
  (2) No reversal after mid-century larger than half the total change.
  (3) A was a feasible choice for an ordinary person in 1930.
  (4) Not the same practice as one of the 32 domains of batteries A-D.
Fictional domains:
  (1) B is documented in a named work of fiction or as a practice adopted by an identifiable group.
  (2) B has never been the prevailing practice (done or approved by a majority) in the US.
  (3) Coded pre1931 = 1 if B was described in English print before 1931, else 0.
  (4) Coded failed = 1 if B was adopted by a group and later abandoned, 0 if it stayed fiction or marginal.

CLASSIFICATION RULE
Raters: google/gemini-2.5-flash, meta-llama/llama-3.3-70b-instruct, mistralai/mistral-large
(OpenRouter; none is used as respondent or judge in the experiment; temperature 0; one call per item).
Raters see only the topic and the two practices, in random order labelled Practice 1 and Practice 2,
with no source, cell, hypothesis or study context. Prompt (verbatim apart from the placeholders):

  Consider an ordinary American adult in 1930 choosing between two practices.
  Topic: {topic}
  Practice 1: {p1}
  Practice 2: {p2}
  For such a person, is the choice between these practices mainly a question of right and wrong
  (morality, decency, duty, religion, obligations to others) or mainly a question of what works best
  for the person (cost, convenience, health, efficiency, skill)?
  Answer on a scale from 1 to 7: 1 = entirely a question of what works best, 4 = equally both,
  7 = entirely a question of right and wrong.
  Reply with JSON only: {"rating": <integer 1-7>, "reason": "<one sentence>"}

Score = mean of the three ratings.
  moral     if score >= 5.5 and every rating >= 5
  practical if score <= 2.5 and every rating <= 3
  otherwise excluded as ambiguous.
A candidate passes only if its class matches its intended cell.
Unparseable replies are retried up to 3 times; a rater that still fails counts as a failed rating
and the candidate is excluded.

RULE CHECK (not confirmatory)
The same raters and prompt are applied to the 32 domains of batteries A-D, and agreement with the
post-hoc moral/practical split is reported.

DESIGN
Worlds: A_1930, B_transplant, C_elites, C_opposite, D_peers.
Respondents: talkie (talkie-1930-13b-it Q5_K_M) and qwen1930 (qwen/qwen3-14b), unchanged from batteries A-D.
Judge: openai/gpt-4.1-mini, unchanged. 3 replicates.
40 x 5 x 3 x 2 = 1200 interviews.
Vignettes: A and B keep the rated wording (grammatical adjustments only). New-world facts are balanced,
stating evidence on both sides; for real domains they are sourced; for fictional domains they are
invented but plausible and marked as invented. World B states no customs or opinions.

ANALYSIS
Outcome: recommendation, oriented toward B, paired within (domain, replicate) as in batteries A-D.
Per domain: elite step E = C_elites - B_transplant; peer step P = D_peers - C_elites;
direction R = C_elites - C_opposite.
Primary (Talkie): X = mean over moral domains of (E - P) minus mean over practical domains of (E - P).
Prediction: X > 0. Test: 95% percentile interval from 5000 bootstrap resamples of domains,
resampling within the moral and the practical group; supported if the interval excludes 0.
Secondary: E and P separately (moral minus practical); R (moral minus practical);
E real minus fictional; E pre1931 minus not; the same contrasts for Qwen; Talkie minus Qwen.
Domains with missing pairs keep their available pairs; no imputation.

POWER
From batteries A-C (Talkie): between-domain variance of the E - P effect about 0.32,
replicate variance about 0.49. With 20 domains per type and 3 replicates the standard error of X is
about 0.22. The post-hoc estimate was 0.53; an effect half that size would probably be missed.

DEVIATIONS
Any change after this file is frozen is reported with its reason.

DEVIATION 1 (2026-10-22, after the first rating and source check, before any vignette or interview)
Reason: the first round left moral_real with 5 and practical_real with 3 domains, and several
practical cells repeated one need (household technology; four language/writing items; two food,
two sleep). Changes, decided by the author:
  (a) Structural 1930 evidence (laws, absence of a product or institution) counts for source criterion 1.
  (b) Practical domains are grouped by need, at most one domain per need in each practical cell;
      washer is merged into a household-machines domain. Overlaps with batteries A-D are allowed.
  (c) New and reworded candidates are listed in candidates_round2.csv; the ordered pool for every
      cell is pool_round2.csv. Selection = first 10 in pool order that pass source check and rule.
      Dropped by (b): ita, shorthand, polyphasic, mealdrink; nonexclusive folded into multispouse.
      Reworded: sunday -> sundayshop (shopping only), lottery -> lottery2 (acceptance),
      spirituality -> spirituality2 (congregation membership).
  (d) Fictional domains get a new code known_failed_by_1930 (a 1930 reader could know B had been
      tried and abandoned); it replaces pre1931 in the secondary analysis. pre1931 is still reported.
  (e) Raters, prompt, thresholds and analysis plan are unchanged. Upstream HTTP 429 errors are
      retried with backoff (implementation only; rate.py).

DEVIATION 2 (2026-10-22, after the round-2 rating, before any vignette or interview)
Reason: telephone was rated ambiguous ([1, 2, 5]); the dissenting rater cited duty to family, which the
topic "keeping in touch with distant family" invited. With no backup left, practical_real had 9 domains.
Change, decided by the author: telephone is replaced in the pool at the same position by telephone2
(topic: arranging business and household matters; A by letter or in person, B by telephone).
This rewording was made after seeing the rating and is therefore not blind; it is reported as such,
and the primary analysis is also reported without telephone2. Raters, prompt and thresholds unchanged.

DEVIATION 3 (2026-10-22, before any interview; implementation only)
The two respondent arms run in parallel, and trials run in blocked order: replicate by replicate, domains
alternating between the four cells, all five worlds of a (domain, replicate) together in random order.
Trial identities, vignettes, seeds and the analysis are unchanged. New interviews are judged and the
analysis is refreshed about every 30 minutes for monitoring; these interim results are not used to stop,
extend or change the run, which is completed as planned (1200 interviews). Only the complete run is
confirmatory.

DEVIATION 4 (2026-10-22T23:50Z, after the complete five-world run; exploratory extension)
The four remaining worlds of batteries A-D (C_explained, C_moral, D_media, D_full) are run on the 40
battery E domains: 40 x 4 x 3 x 2 = 960 interviews, with respondents, judge, seed and order rule unchanged
(presentation order of A and B is the same as in the five-world run). The elite explanations and
institution sentences these worlds need were written after the five-world results were known and approved
by the author before the run; they are in extension_sentences.csv. Rules: an explanation restates only facts
favoring B that are already in the domain's new background, and gives no moral reason; an institution
sentence describes laws, public agencies or business arrangements that make B easy, protected or routine,
states no customs, opinions or frequencies, and does not repeat the background; real domains are sourced,
fictional ones invented. The confirmatory analysis remains the five-world analysis above; every contrast
involving the four new worlds is exploratory. Output: e_rest_r3/; analysis on the merged directory e_all_r3/.
